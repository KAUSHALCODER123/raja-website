Creation Memories Photography is a Kolkata-based studio for people who want photographs that feel real. We photograph weddings from the first haldi splash to the final bidaai, and we bring the same eye to corporate stages, brand launches and product studios.

Our approach is documentary at heart and editorial in finish. We let moments unfold, then shape every frame in our own edit suite with careful colour, retouching and cinematic films, so what you receive looks like the day felt.
